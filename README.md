# 🌱 HabitSeen — Daily Habits & Willpower Tracker

> **Master your habits, build unbreakable discipline, and live with clarity.**

HabitSeen is your personal habit companion designed to help you stay consistent, build daily momentum, and track your life's progress without overwhelming clutter or toxic guilt.

Whether you're building a workout routine, drinking more water, reading every night, learning to code, or simply staying mindful — HabitSeen makes every small win feel visible, rewarding, and deeply personal.

---

## ✨ Why You'll Love HabitSeen

### 🎯 Daily Habits & Micro-Wins
- **Clean, focused task views**: Switch between Grid and Horizontal list views anytime.
- **Drag & Drop Reordering**: Hold and arrange your habits in the exact priority order you want for your day.
- **Custom Color Accents**: Pick vibrant colors that make each habit card uniquely yours.
- **Interactive Calendar Strip**: Quickly navigate across days and track your week at a glance.

### 📊 "Just Tracking" Flexible Mode
- Have habits you just want to log casually without streak pressure?
- The dedicated **Just Tracking** space allows you to record habits freely without streak breaks, penalty timers, or lost hearts.

### ⚡ Gamified Discipline & Willpower XP
- Earn **Willpower XP** for every habit you complete.
- Complete **100% of your daily habits** to earn bonus XP and climb from *Habit Initiate* to legendary discipline ranks.
- Visual streak chronometers keep your long-term focus locked in.

### ❤️ Vitality (Hearts) & Account Security
- Start with 5 vitality hearts. Stay consistent to unlock up to 50 lives!
- Set a personal **Security Question & Password** so your habits and account data remain securely protected.

### 🎬 Discipline Fuel Reels
- Need a 30-second mindset reset?
- Watch curated motivational shorts directly inside the app with full audio mute controls and smart 2-hour shuffle rotation.

### 🔄 In-App Updates & Cloud Releases
- **One-Tap Live Updates**: Whenever new features or fixes are pushed, you can update directly inside the app with a live progress bar.
- **GitHub Cloud Releases**: Every version is automatically built and published as a ready-to-install Android APK.
